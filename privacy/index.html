Who we are

What we collect

The public homepage does not include a contact form. Information is collected when you do business with the shop, including:

  • At check-in (including on the in-store intake tool): your name, email, phone number, card details, photos of cards, the services you asked for (for example grading, prep, evaluation, repair or auction), and your agreement to the submission terms
  • On the customer status page: the lookup details needed to show your order (such as the status link or ticket information you were given)
  • In person at the shop, as needed to complete a sale, buy a collection, or process a drop-off

How that information is handled

  • Intake and order records are stored in a Supabase database used by the shop’s staff tools and the customer status page.
  • If you drop off cards for prep, a pre-grade evaluation, or repair, the shop may open a work order with Restored Rarity TCG and share the customer and card details needed to do that work. This is described on the site and in the terms you sign at check-in.
  • The homepage loads fonts from Google Fonts and embeds a Google Map for directions.
  • This site does not currently include a visitor-analytics or advertising pixel. If that changes, this page should be updated.
