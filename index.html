Get your cards graded

No account, no shipping, no forms to mail. Drop your cards off at PBG and we handle the paperwork, packing and shipping to the grader.

  1. Drop off your cards

    We check each card in on our iPad, photograph it and give you a ticket number.

  2. Add a professional evaluation or prep

    We send cards to our partner, Restored Rarity TCG, where restoration professionals evaluate and prep them at PBG partner pricing.

  3. We submit to the grader

    Your cards go out with our next submission at the service level you picked.

  4. Track it, then pick up your slabs

    Follow every grading stage from the link on your receipt. Bring your ticket number and a photo ID to pick up. Storage is free for 30 days after we tell you your cards are ready; after that a $1 per card per day holding fee applies.

We submit to PSA, CGC, Beckett (BGS) and TAG. Grading prices are the company's current fee plus a $20 PBG handling fee per card. Cards worth over $4,000 get our gem service, with a magnetic case to display the slab: $40 over $4,000, $60 over $6,000, $80 over $8,000 and $100 over $10,000. Want your slab ready to display? Add a matching magnetic slab case for $15 a card with any grading submission, and we'll hand it to you with your slab at pickup. Ask at the counter for service levels and turnaround.

Already dropped off? Scan the QR code on your receipt to see which grading stage your cards are in.

Partner pricing with Restored Rarity TCG

Your cards are evaluated and prepped by professionals in the card restoration space who work to the requirements of PSA, CGC, Beckett and TAG. Drop your cards off at PBG and we send them over, so you pay the partner rate and never ship them yourself.

  • Full evaluation with magnification $17 per cardA professional pre-grader inspects centering, corners, edges and surface under magnification, then tells you what grade range to expect and whether the card is worth the grading fee.
  • Prep $7 per cardCleaning and grading prep by a restoration professional who knows what each grading company accepts, so your card goes in looking its best.
Authorized Restored Rarity drop-offNeed a card repaired or restored? PBG is an authorized drop-off location for Restored Rarity. Check it in at the counter and it goes straight to Restored Rarity, who send you their own quote and tracking link.
